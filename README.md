# Super Store Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Super Store Sales Dashboard**
designed to analyze sales and profitability across regions, time
periods, product categories, customer segments, payment modes, shipping
modes, and sub-categories.

The dashboard provides a high-level view of business performance while
allowing users to filter results by **Central, East, South, and West
regions**.

## 🎯 Dashboard KPIs

  KPI                                   Value
  --------------------------- ---------------
  **Total Sales**                 **\$1.60M**
  **Total Profit**              **\$175.26K**
  **Average Delivery Time**     **3.93 days**

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Profit by Month and Year

The dashboard compares monthly profit trends for **2019 and 2020**,
making it possible to identify changes in profitability throughout the
year.

### 2. Sales by Month and Year

Monthly sales are compared across 2019 and 2020 to understand sales
growth and seasonal patterns.

### 3. Profit by Category

-   **Technology:** \$90K
-   **Office Supplies:** \$75K
-   **Furniture:** \$10K

Technology is the strongest profit-generating category in the dashboard.

### 4. Profit by Customer Segment

-   **Consumer:** 46%
-   **Corporate:** 33%
-   **Home Office:** 21%

The Consumer segment contributes the largest share of profit.

### 5. Profit by Payment Mode

-   **COD:** 47%
-   **Online:** 31%
-   **Cards:** 22%

COD represents the largest share of profit among the displayed payment
modes.

### 6. Returns by Ship Mode

The dashboard shows return counts by shipping method: - **Standard
Class:** 3.5K - **Second Class:** 1.1K - **First Class:** 1.0K - **Same
Day:** 0.3K

Standard Class has the highest number of returns in the dashboard.

### 7. Profit by Sub-Category

Top displayed sub-categories include: - **Copiers:** \$43K -
**Accessories:** \$25K - **Phones:** \$22K - **Paper:** \$21K -
**Binders:** \$18K - **Storage:** \$14K - **Chairs:** \$13K -
**Appliances:** \$13K

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

-   The business generated **\$1.60M in total sales** and **\$175.26K in
    total profit**.
-   **Technology** is the leading profit-generating category at
    **\$90K**, followed by Office Supplies at **\$75K**.
-   **Consumer customers contribute 46% of profit**, making them the
    largest customer segment.
-   **COD accounts for 47% of profit**, higher than Online (31%) and
    Cards (22%).
-   **Standard Class has the highest return count at 3.5K**,
    substantially above the other shipping modes shown.
-   **Copiers generate the highest displayed sub-category profit at
    \$43K**.
-   The dashboard compares **2019 vs. 2020** monthly sales and profit
    trends to highlight changes in business performance over time.
